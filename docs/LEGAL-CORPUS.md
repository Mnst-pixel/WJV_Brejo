# Legal corpus

Kairós never claims complete national coverage without measurements. The coverage dashboard records source, jurisdiction, organ, document type, period, counts, last successful retrieval, failures, licensing/reuse status, and reviewer confidence.

Each legal item distinguishes original text, official source, immutable version, temporal validity, exam reference date, historical official answer, current approved law, supersession, retrieval time, document hash, and human approval.

Public availability does not establish reuse rights. Every source registry entry requires copyright/licensing status and an approval basis before redistribution.

The supplied legacy HTML is extracted only into `legacy_unverified`; it is never represented as legally approved. The non-executing extractor reconciled 101 questions across four datasets, 14 OAB summary sections, 95 summary items, 20 functional panels, and 15 local-storage concepts. JSON, Markdown, and CSV outputs live in `legacy/extracted/`; the original stays outside Git until redistribution rights are established and is preserved on the VPS under `/opt/kairos/legacy/`.
